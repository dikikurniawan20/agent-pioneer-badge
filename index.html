How to mint

Two ways to get your badge. Pick the one that suits you.

Manual — mint it yourself

  1. Connect your wallet above (injected provider — the wallet you already use).
  2. The site reads your Agent ID balance on-chain. Nothing is signed for this check.
  3. If you hold an Agent ID and have not minted, press Mint — your wallet shows the transaction for you to confirm.
  4. Your badge appears on the wall below and in your wallet. That is all.

With your AI agent

  1. Copy the prompt in the section below.
  2. Paste it to your AI agent — the one that can transact from your wallet.
  3. Your agent checks your Agent ID, prepares the mint, and shows you the transaction first.
  4. You approve it, the agent sends it, and reports your badge number and transaction hash.

Privacy: this site stores nothing on a server. It only talks to the chain through your wallet and a public read-only node. It never asks for your seed phrase, private key, or password — anyone who does is a scam.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. The mint function costs nothing — you only pay the network gas fee for the transaction. There is no mint price, no hidden fee, and no payment to anyone.

How many badges can I mint?

One per wallet. The contract enforces it on-chain — a second mint from the same wallet will fail.

What do I need to mint?

A wallet that holds an Agent ID. The contract checks your Agent ID balance on-chain before it lets you mint. If you do not hold one, the mint will fail.

What is the badge for?

It is a commemorative keepsake — a record that you held an Agent ID in the early days. It has no utility, no promised returns, and no airdrop attached to it.

Where is the contract?

On BSC mainnet at 0xdf72e80ec7d3266d754050391508fba914168717. The source is open and verified on-chain.
